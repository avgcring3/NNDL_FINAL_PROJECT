NNDL Capstone

RideFlow NN

Neural taxi demand forecasting for better dispatch and city mobility planning.

Problem

Taxi supply is often in the wrong place at the wrong hour. Busy zones get long waits and missed rides, while quiet zones keep drivers idle.

Data

  • Reproducible operational-style Moscow zone demand.
  • Dynamic hourly history ending at the current Moscow hour.
  • Peak, airport, weekend, and event demand patterns.
  • CSV and trip-log adapters for future real-data retraining.

Modeling Approach

  • Seasonal naive and MLP baselines.
  • Transformer encoder over historical demand windows.
  • Zone embedding for local behavior.
  • Weighted MAE loss for peak-hour demand.

KPI And Success

MAE/RMSE
Improve forecast error over same-hour-yesterday baseline.

Peak Demand
Reduce under-forecasting in high-demand zone-hours.

Operations
Improve repositioning and reduce idle driver hours.

Impact
Shorter passenger wait time and more completed rides.
